# U106 — windglyph-concepten

Gemaakt met de ingebouwde `image_gen`-tool, 2026-11-09. Ontwerpverkenning; geen implementatie of productbesluit.

Alle gekozen beelden zijn visueel bekeken: links voorbeelden bij 2 Bft, 5 Bft en 8 Bft; rechts twaalf kaartsymbolen met uiteenlopende richtingen en leesbare cijfers. De gegenereerde kaarten zijn illustratief. De generator tekent de symbolen groter dan de gevraagde 28–40 px: de bladen bewijzen dus nog geen leesbaarheid op echte telefoonmaat. Materiaaltextuur en gloed vragen bij uitwerking vereenvoudiging.

Voorkeur voor verder onderzoek: **papier** (eenvoudige kleurvlakken, donker cijfer, duidelijke staart) en **windwijzer** (donker silhouet, expliciete pijlpunt, rustig cijfer). Bij de windwijzer moet de conventie voor windrichting expliciet worden vastgelegd; dit blad toont de pijl met de stroming mee. Dit is een ontwerpobservatie, geen productbesluit.

## 01 — gravure

Bestand: `concept-01-gravure.png`

Exacte prompt van het gekozen beeld:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wind-map glyph design exploration for motregen, a Dutch weather app. Create ONE landscape concept board, 1536x1024 composition. Left 40 percent: three large isolated examples vertically, labelled only by integrated upright Beaufort numerals 2, 5, 8. Right 60 percent: a quiet modern light map of the Netherlands with recognizable coast, pale blue sea, pale sage/sand land, subtle waterways, no map labels, around twelve miniature versions of exactly the same glyph family, in varied directions and strengths 1 through 9. Miniatures must really be small, about 28–40 px tall, not giant map decorations. Each glyph must have a strong directional silhouette, short drooping tail at 2, moderately extended at 5, taut longer at 8; numeral stays upright regardless of orientation. Strong legible dark single-digit numerals on a clean light integral badge. Beauty from European handcrafted workmanship combined with restrained digital magic. Design for clarity at phone scale; decoration subordinate to silhouette. Only visible text is Beaufort digits; no headings, legends, words, logos, compass letters, or watermarks. Clean generous spacing. No interface chrome. 
Critical map layout: place twelve SMALL glyphs in a loose 3-column by 4-row distribution, each miniature only 32 pixels high on this full board. Rotate their tails to point in twelve visibly DIFFERENT headings: north, northeast, east, southeast, south, southwest, west, northwest, north-northeast, east-southeast, south-southwest, west-northwest. Rotate the whole directional silhouette, but keep every digit upright and readable; do NOT make all tails point right. Avoid oversized badges on map.
Style direction: Dutch nautical copperplate engraving: an elegant engraved windsock/pennant with navy ink contour and warm ochre panels, restrained fine hatching visible only in large examples, tiny brass mouth ring with ivory number medallion. Digital magic: a hairline cyan luminous current following the tail, two tiny light flecks. Keep miniature silhouette bold and spare, not antique map background.
```
